# Moon surface

`moon-lroc-2k.jpg` is the unmodified 2048 × 1024 colour map from
[NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio — CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/).

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio. Visualizer: Ernie Wright (USRA).
Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter / LROC WAC colour mosaic; December 2025 map.

Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg

The application projects the map onto a sphere and applies neutral silver shading.
